LAFD AI Chat Bot


Overview:
• Developed an AI-powered chatbot for the Los Angeles Fire Department (LAFD) Help Desk, streamlining service request management by automating call handling and reducing bottlenecks during high-demand periods

• Integrated Microsoft Azure Cognitive Services and Communication APIs to enable advanced speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and call automation functionalities

• Implemented a modular, event-driven architecture, allowing independent development and testing of call flow components for improved scalability and maintainability

• Collaborated with stakeholders (LAFD IT Department) to align project deliverables with organizational needs, enhancing internal operations and employee satisfaction through efficient service workflows
